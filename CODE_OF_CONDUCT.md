# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate in public and private project spaces.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and accept responsibility when you make a mistake.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, and other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that do not follow this Code of Conduct. To report an incident, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's organization profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
